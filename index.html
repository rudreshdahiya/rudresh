Rudresh Dahiya

Hi! Welcome to my digital playground.

I’m Rudresh, a Product Manager at Anand Rathi Group – Lxme, building a wealth platform for first-generation women investors.

Most of my work starts with a why. Why aren’t SIPs actually running? Which farmer actually farmed better? Why does a shop’s first online sale take so long? Each answer has led me to the next question, with Walmart × Flipkart, HUL, the World Bank, SNCF and a pickle brand I started in rural Bihar.

This is where I keep the messy drafts, case studies and experiments that come out of those questions. The longer story is on the me page.

I use GenAI and agentic tools to think faster, not to think for me. I write about what I’m learning on Substack, you can read it here.

Projects

UPI Autopay before SIP

Why weren’t SIPs actually running? Most new SIPs never finished the eNACH mandate, so nothing debited. Swapped in one-tap UPI Autopay. More SIPs went live, and AUM grew. Lxme • 2025 • Conversion

Walmart Vriddhi

Why is the first online sale so slow? Offline shopkeepers didn’t know how Flipkart works. Trained them to list and position products. First sales came faster, and order values rose. Walmart × Flipkart • 2022–23 • Growth

Let’s Pickle!

Why is the surplus going to waste? Farmers’ extra produce was thrown out or sold cheap. Turned it into pickles with SEWA Bharat’s women’s collective. Recovered capital, won 3M-CII. SEWA Bharat • 2020–21 • 0→1 & P&L

Station Demand Study

What does a busy station actually need? Surveyed commuters on what was missing and how they travel. Top asks: healthy food, a medicine shop and a shuttle to the nearest metro. SNCF • 2019–20 • User research

Self-serve KYC

Why does KYC need our team on the phone? Account opening meant our team chasing users across several check-ins. Rebuilt it so users finish KYC themselves, in one sitting. Lxme • 2025 • Activation

Farmer Payouts

Which farmer actually farmed better? Payouts ran on surveys, which are opinions. Satellite pixels changing colour showed every sowing and harvest, so payouts followed facts. HUL • 2022–23 • Metric design

Bus-Space Freight

Is there an empty seat for our parcels? Freight was eating our margins. Not every RedBus seat gets booked, so empty space on intercity buses became our freight, far cheaper than couriers. SBI YFI • 2020–21 • Unit economics

Education Outcomes

What actually gets a student a job? Across schools in Odisha, compared marks, attendance and teaching with who got hired. Co-curriculars and teacher training mattered most. Lend A Hand India • 2019–20 • Impact
